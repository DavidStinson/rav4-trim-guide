Interior & Comfort
Modern Cabin Designed with you in mind, RAV4’s interior makes comfort feel effortless. From intuitive controls within easy reach to space that adapts to your needs, RAV4 is ready to amplify your day, no matter where you go.
Power Tilt/Slide Panoramic Glass Roof Let fresh air and natural light fill every seat with RAV4’s available panoramic glass roof that features power tilt and slide capability.
Power-Adjustable SofTex-Trimmed Seats Quick and comfortable seating. Both the driver and front passenger can customize their ideal seat positions with RAV4’s available 8-way power-adjustable front seats, and the driver’s seat also features memory settings, for added convenience.
Heated Rear Seats Warm up the whole crew with RAV4’s available heated rear outboard seats, designed for chilly days and cozy nights.
Heated and Ventilated Front Seats Whether it’s cool mornings or hot afternoons, available heated and ventilated front seats keep you feeling just right.
Woodland Interior Design Inspired by trail-ready gear, Woodland’s vibrant orange stitching brings the spirit of the wild to the seats, steering wheel and center console lid. Distinct patterns on the door and dash, plus a side dash compartment, make every drive feel like a call to adventure.
Cargo Capacity Room to pack. Freedom to play. With easy-fold 60/40 rear seats and up to 70.4 cu. ft. of cargo space with the rear seats folded down, RAV4 delivers the versatility to pack whatever the trail—or the tailgate—calls for.
All-Weather Floor and Cargo Mats Ready for sand, mud and everything in between, RAV4 Woodland’s all-weather floor and cargo mats help keep your cabin adventure-ready with outdoor-inspired, Woodland-exclusive designs.

Performance & Capability
Woodland Suspension and All-Terrain Tires From the paved road to far off the beaten path, Woodland’s standard 18-in. wheels and taller all-terrain tires deliver confident traction. Paired with a unique suspension, you get an additional 1-in. ground clearance to take your adventures even higher.
Multi-Terrain Select No matter the path you take, RAV4’s Multi-Terrain Select (MTS) acts as your off-road copilot. Standard for Woodland and available for other trims when equipped with AWD, it automatically balances the throttle and braking to deliver maximum grip on rock, dirt, mud or sand.
3D Multi-Terrain Monitor Whether it’s dirt, gravel or an uneven path, Woodland’s 3D Multi-Terrain Monitor helps you see what’s underfoot, so you can push forward with confidence.
Raised Roof Rails With Cross Bars Load up and head out with ease. Proudly bearing the Woodland badge, RAV4 Woodland’s standard raised roof rails with cross bars give you more ways to bring your gear along.
Advanced Hybrid System With RAV4's hybrid technology and its impressive fuel efficiency, you're ready to chase new experiences with confidence, near or far.
Impressive Towing Capacity Tow trailers, gear and more with a maximum 3500 lbs. of towing capacity on Woodland.
Front-Wheel Drive Available on LE, SE and XLE Premium, RAV4's Front-Wheel Drive helps enhance efficiency without sacrificing its athletic feel.

Traffic Jam Assist This available feature helps you navigate the ebb and flow of stop-and-go traffic on controlled-access highways by providing temporary hands-free control of the vehicle at speeds under 25 mph, provided the driver remains alert and ready to resume control when conditions change or the system requests intervention.

Safety and Convenience
Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert RAV4’s Blind Spot Monitor (BSM) is designed to warn you when a vehicle enters your blind spot and Rear Cross-Traffic Alert (RCTA) helps detect vehicles approaching from the sides as you reverse. Additionally, Safe Exit Alert (SEA) helps alert passengers of approaching vehicles or cyclists when a door is opening.
Panoramic View Monitor With 3D View The available Panoramic View Monitor (PVM) with 3D view helps you see multiple angles as you navigate tight turns, narrow spaces and tricky parking.
Drive Recorder Dash Cam From unexpected moments to everyday drives, the standard Drive Recorder Dash Cam helps document portions of your journey by capturing front and rear video during key moments on the road.
Eight Airbags RAV4 comes standard with eight airbags total, including a driver and front passenger Advanced Airbag System with driver and front passenger seat-mounted side airbags, driver knee airbag, a front passenger seat-cushion airbag, and front and rear side curtain airbags.
Star Safety System™ Every new RAV4 comes with our Star Safety System™, an integration of safety technologies designed to enhance traction, braking and more. Armed with these safety features, RAV4 is designed to help keep you out of harm’s way.
No Cost Maintenance Plan and Roadside Assistance Every new Toyota comes with ToyotaCare, a maintenance plan that covers normal factory scheduled service for 1 year or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first and 24-hour Roadside Assistance for 2 years, unlimited mileage.
Toyota Safety Sense™ 4.0
Pre-Collision System With Pedestrian Detection PCS w/PD is designed to alert you to potential collisions and may automatically brake.
Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control DRCC is designed to maintain a preset distance from the vehicle ahead of you, from highway speeds down to a full stop.
Lane Departure Alert With Steering Assist LDA w/SA is designed to alert you to inadvertent lane departures and make minor steering corrections to help keep you in your lane.
Lane Tracing Assist LTA is designed to center the vehicle within its lane while DRCC is active.
Road Sign Assist RSA is designed to recognize certain road signs and display them to you inside the vehicle.
Automatic High Beams AHB is designed to automatically toggle between high and low beams based on the vehicle’s surroundings.
Proactive Driving Assist PDA is designed to provide gentle braking and/or steering to help control distance between your vehicle and a vehicle, pedestrian or bicyclist ahead of you.
