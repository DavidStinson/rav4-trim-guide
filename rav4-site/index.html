Four 2027 RAV4s, side by side

Three are the RAV4 Hybrid. They share the engine, all-wheel drive and body, and differ in comfort, cameras and looks. The fourth, the Plug-in Hybrid XSE, has a bigger battery you charge from an outlet, so short trips can run on electricity alone.

Every price on this page includes Toyota's $1,595 destination fee. None include tax or registration. Gas mileage: 41 mpg (XLE Premium), 40 mpg (XSE and Limited), combined. The Plug-in XSE goes about 50 miles on a charge, then gets 39 mpg.
